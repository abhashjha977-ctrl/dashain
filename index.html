01

The feeling underneath

A victory
you can touch.

Dashain is a long exhale. Across Nepal, the biggest festival of the year turns roads into reunion routes and houses into bright, generous stages.

It remembers the triumph of good over evil, but it is lived through the intimate: a forehead pressed to an elder’s hand, green shoots tucked behind an ear, a new shirt waiting for its first day out.
